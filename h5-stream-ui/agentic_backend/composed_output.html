Trip Overview

Experience the Best of Hangzhou

Start your morning at the sacred Lingyin Temple surrounded by ancient stone carvings, spend the afternoon cycling along West Lake's poetic causeways past iconic sights like Broken Bridge and Leifeng Pagoda, and end your evening savoring authentic Hangzhou cuisine at a lakeside restaurant or exploring the vibrant Hefang Street night market.

Day Itinerary

Visit Lingyin Temple (灵隐寺)

Lingyin Temple, Hangzhou

Arrive before 8 AM to avoid crowds. Free from Dec 2025. Take Bus #7 from Longxiangqiao Station or Bus #1314. Metro Line 3 to Huanglong Sports Center then special line. Avoid driving!

Explore Feilai Peak stone carvings

Feilai Peak, Hangzhou

Follow the stream path for the best carvings. No photos inside halls. Dress modestly. Don't step on thresholds.

Enjoy vegetarian noodles

Lingyin Temple vegetarian hall, Hangzhou

Famous vegetarian noodles — highly recommended. ~¥20-30 per bowl. No photos inside halls.

Optional visit to Yongfu Temple

Yongfu Temple, Hangzhou

Free with Feilin Temple ticket. 'No.1 Blessed Place in Qiantang' — quieter, beautiful gardens.

Cycle along Su Causeway (苏堤春晓)

Su Causeway, Hangzhou

Rent a shared bike (~¥20/hour). Ideal for cycling or walking. Pair with Longjing tea at a lakeside teahouse.

Visit Leifeng Pagoda (雷峰塔)

Leifeng Pagoda, Hangzhou

Take the elevator to the top for panoramic views. Best time: dusk for golden sunset views. Avoid photos in temple halls.
