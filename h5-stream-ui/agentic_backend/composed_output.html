Trip Overview

Hangzhou (杭州), Zhejiang Province, China

Experience the best of Hangzhou in one day: start your morning at the sacred Lingyin Temple surrounded by ancient stone carvings, spend the afternoon cycling along West Lake's poetic causeways past iconic sights like Broken Bridge and Leifeng Pagoda, and end your evening savoring authentic Hangzhou cuisine at a lakeside restaurant or exploring the vibrant Hefang Street night market.

Date August 10, 2026 (summer, peak lotus season)
Weather Hot and humid with temperatures 28–35°C (highs), 23–27°C (lows). Mostly cloudy with afternoon thunderstorms. Frequent rain. Typhoon 'White Dolphin' passed through around Aug 5–9, bringing heavy rain relief. High humidity (80–95%). UV Index: Very high (10–11). Travel tip: Bring an umbrella AND sunscreen. Start outdoor activities before 11 AM to avoid afternoon storms.

Top Scenic Spots

Three Pools Mirroring the Moon

The most iconic of the 'Ten Scenes of West Lake'

The most iconic of the 'Ten Scenes of West Lake' — the image printed on the back of China's 1 RMB banknote. Three stone pagodas rise from the water; the interplay of moonlight and reflections is magical.

Estimated visit duration

1–1.5 hours

Leifeng Pagoda

Famous from the White Snake legend

Famous from the White Snake legend. Take the elevator to the top for panoramic views of the entire West Lake. Sunset here is legendary — golden light spills across the lake.

Estimated visit duration

1–1.5 hours

Broken Bridge

The legendary meeting place of the White Snake Lady and Xu Xian

The legendary meeting place of the White Snake Lady and Xu Xian. Famous for winter snow scenes but photogenic year-round.

Estimated visit duration

30–40 minutes

Su Causeway

A 2.8km poetic causeway lined with weeping willows and peach blossoms in spring

A 2.8km poetic causeway lined with weeping willows and peach blossoms in spring. Ideal for cycling or walking.

Estimated visit duration

1–2 hours (walking) / 30–40 min (cycling)
