| Dish | Chinese | Description |

## Fine Dining (¥¥¥)

### Four Seasons — Jinsha Hall (金沙厅)
- **Rating**: Michelin 2 Stars, Black Pearl 3 Diamonds
- **Price**: ~¥757/person
- **Location**: 5 Lingyin Road
- **Must-Try**: Jinsha Crispy Chicken, Braised Abalone with Pork, Longjing Tea Stewed Milk, Song Sao Fish Soup
- **Tip**: Reserve at least 2 weeks in advance. Nine seasonal menus rotate yearly.

### West Lake State Guesthouse — Ziwai Hall (紫薇厅)
- **Rating**: "Gold standard" for West Lake Vinegar Fish
- **Price**: ~¥475/person
- **Location**: 18 Yanggong Causeway, Building 8
- **Must-Try**: West Lake Vinegar Fish, Tiger Spring Water Matsutake Rice, Dongpo Braised Pork
- **Tip**: Request window-side seat. Reserve days ahead for weekends.

### 28 Hubin Road (湖滨28中餐厅)
- **Rating**: Michelin Plate 2024 & 2025, #43 OAD Asia
- **Price**: ~¥400-558/person
- **Location**: Grand Hyatt Hangzhou, 1F, 28 Hubin Road
- **Must-Try**: Signature Braised Pork Belly (pagoda-shaped, 19 layers), Meigancai Braised Beef Tongue
- **Tip**: Easy to book. All-day hours 9 AM–10 PM.

### Ru Yuan (如院)
- **Rating**: Tatler Best 100 Asia-Pacific 2025
- **Location**: Inside Hangzhou Botanical Garden near West Lake
- **Must-Try**: Poached marble goby with sweet vinegar sauce, Sautéed river shrimps with Longjing tea noodles

## Mid-Range (¥¥) — Local Favorites

### Green Tea Restaurant (绿茶餐厅)
- **Price**: ~¥80-120/person
- **Location**: 83 Longjing Road, Xihu District
- **Hours**: 10 AM–10 PM daily
- **Must-Try**: Green Tea Roast Chicken, Braised Dongpo Pork, Longjing Tea Shrimp, Sweet & Sour Ribs
- **Ambiance**: Set in serene tea plantation surroundings — excellent value and ambiance

### Zhiweiguan (知味观)
- **Price**: Moderate
- **Location**: 83 Ren He Road, Hubin area (steps from West Lake)
- **Must-Try**: Xiaolongbao (soup dumplings), seasonal hairy crab, wonton soup, chive dumplings
- **Vibe**: Iconic time-honored Hangzhou institution with lively atmosphere

### Longtangli (龙堂里)
- **Price**: ~¥100-150/person
- **Location**: 18 Longjing Road, Xihu Subdistrict
- **Must-Try**: Scallion bun stew, pork belly, shrimp with garlic paste and vermicelli
- **Ambiance**: Near Maojiabu, surrounded by greenery

### Hu Qing Yu Tang Yao Shan (胡庆余堂药膳)
- **Rating**: Michelin Plate 2024 & 2025
- **Price**: Mid-tier
- **Location**: 146-1 Nanshan Road, Shangcheng District
- **Must-Try**: Huang qi river shrimps, Steamed fu ling dumplings
- **Concept**: Herbal medicine-inspired restorative cuisine

### Jiangnan Mingshi Yuan (江南名石苑)
- **Price**: ~¥121/person
- **Location**: 89 Beishan Road (inside Quyuan Fenghe park)
- **Must-Try**: Pickled fish with sour cabbage, pepper chicken, iron-plate sea bass
- **Bonus**: Beautiful West Lake night views

## Budget-Friendly (¥)

### Qiantang Kitchen (钱塘厨房)
- **Price**: ~¥113/person
- **Location**: 9 Fenghuang Mountain Road (inside Hangzhou Cuisine Museum)
- **Must-Try**: Dongpo Braised Pork, West Lake Vinegar Fish, Song Sao Fish Soup, Crab Roe Tofu

### Deming Restaurant (德明饭店)
- **Price**: ~¥120/person
- **Location**: 36 Huyu Road
- **Must-Try**: Crispy Large Intestines, Qiantang River White Bass, Rich Braised Pork
- **Vibe**: Hangzhou's quintessential "fly restaurant" (hole-in-the-wall), decades of fame

### Baxian Gohai (八仙够嗨)
- **Price**: ~¥98/person
- **Location**: 36 Huyu Road (same alley as Deming)
- **Must-Try**: Home-style Rice Cake with Small Yellow Fish, Crispy Large Intestines, Pickled Chili Bullfrog

### Haimao Canteen (海猫食堂)
- **Price**: ~¥132/person
- **Location**: At foot of Phoenix Mountain (near old Southern Song imperial city)
- **Must-Try**: Steamed Yongchang Stinky Tofu with Chopped Chili, Sour Soup Bullfrog
- **Bonus**: Seasonal menu + good craft beer selection

## Best Dining Seasons
- **Spring (late March–April)**: Longjing tea harvest — restaurants feature tea-infused dishes
- **Autumn**: Hairy crab season — freshwater crab preparations peak
