# Hangzhou One-Day Trip — Overview

## Destination
- **City**: Hangzhou (杭州), Zhejiang Province, China
- **UNESCO Status**: West Lake Cultural Landscape — UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2011
- **Best for**: One-day trip, cultural heritage, natural scenery, tea culture

## Date
- **Trip date**: August 10, 2026 (summer, peak lotus season)
- **Day type**: Sunday (weekend — expect crowds, arrive early)

## Weather — August 2026 (Current)
- **Temperature**: 28–35°C (highs), 23–27°C (lows) — hot and humid
- **Conditions**: Mostly cloudy with afternoon thunderstorms. Frequent rain.
- **Pattern**: Hot sunny mornings followed by afternoon thundershowers (typical summer pattern)
- **Typhoon note**: Typhoon "White Dolphin" passed through around Aug 5–9, bringing heavy rain relief. Temperatures moderating to 28–35°C range.
- **Humidity**: High (80–95%)
- **UV Index**: Very high (10–11) — sunscreen essential
- **Sunrise/Sunset**: ~5:20 AM / ~6:50 PM
- **Travel tip**: Bring an umbrella AND sunscreen. Start outdoor activities before 11 AM to avoid afternoon storms. Misty, drizzly West Lake is classically beautiful.

## Weather — Seasonal Overview
- **Spring (Mar–May)**: 15–25°C, cherry blossoms, tea harvest season, occasional rain
- **Summer (Jun–Aug)**: 28–35°C, lotus flowers in full bloom, humid, afternoon thunderstorms. **Current season.**
- **Autumn (Sep–Nov)**: 18–28°C, clear skies, osmanthus fragrance, best season
- **Winter (Dec–Feb)**: 0–10°C, occasional snow (magical on West Lake)

## Trip Overview (2-3 sentences)
Experience the best of Hangzhou in one day: start your morning at the sacred Lingyin Temple surrounded by ancient stone carvings, spend the afternoon cycling along West Lake's poetic causeways past iconic sights like Broken Bridge and Leifeng Pagoda, and end your evening savoring authentic Hangzhou cuisine at a lakeside restaurant or exploring the vibrant Hefang Street night market.
