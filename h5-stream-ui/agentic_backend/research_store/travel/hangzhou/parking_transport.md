## Smart Parking Tips

- **App**: Download "西湖智慧出行" (West Lake Smart Travel) or WeChat mini-program "西湖停车" for real-time space availability (red/yellow/green, updates every 5 min)
- **Avoid driving into the core area**: Peak parking shortage reaches 65%! Use Metro + Bus.
- **Best arrival time**: Before 8:30 AM on weekdays
- **Avoid peak hours**: 9:00–11:00 AM and 3:00–5:00 PM on weekends
- **Holidays**: Pre-book via "杭州交警" (Hangzhou Traffic Police) app
- **Emergency**: Scenic area dispatch center ☎ 0571-87071111

## Key Transport Tips

1. **Metro + Bus combo is best**: Avoid traffic and parking headaches entirely
2. **Shared bikes**: Must park within white lines — fines of ¥50 for improper parking in West Lake area
3. **Electric sightseeing carts**: ¥40/person around the lake if you want to save energy
4. **Weekend driving restrictions**: The scenic area has restrictions — check before driving
5. **Boat**: From Hubin Park or Yigongyuan Pier to Three Pools — ¥55/person round trip
