# Hangzhou Travel Tips

## Best Seasons to Visit
- **🌸 Spring (Mar–May)**: Cherry blossoms, tea harvest, best weather (15–25°C). Most photogenic season.
- **☀️ Summer (Jun–Aug)**: Hot (28–35°C) but stunning lotus blooms. Best for Curved Yard and Lotus Pool.
- **🍂 Autumn (Sep–Nov)**: Clear skies, golden foliage, osmanthus fragrance. Best overall season.
- **❄️ Winter (Dec–Feb)**: Cold (0–10°C) but magical if it snows. "Lingering Snow on Broken Bridge" is a must-see.

## What to Bring
- ☂️ **Umbrella** — Hangzhou weather changes quickly. Drizzly West Lake is classic!
- 👟 **Comfortable walking shoes** — You'll walk 15,000–25,000 steps
- 🧴 **Sunscreen + hat** — UV is stronger near water and in mountains
- 🦟 **Mosquito repellent** — Especially in summer. BUT: don't use near Lingyin Temple
- 🧥 **Light jacket** — Even in spring, mountain areas are cooler
- 📱 **Power bank** — For navigation apps, photo taking, and bike rental apps
- 🫗 **Water bottle** — Drinking water stations available at scenic spots
- 👗 **Light-colored clothing** — Photographs best against green tea gardens

## Best Photo Spots
- 📸 **Leifeng Pagoda at sunset** — Golden light spilling across West Lake like "orange soda"
- 📸 **Su Causeway at dawn** — Empty causeway, mist on the water, willows in morning light
- 📸 **Flower Harbor stone bridge** — Frame koi fish + Leifeng Pagoda in one shot (use ultra-wide 0.5× lens)
- 📸 **Curved Yard Lotus Pool** — Red buildings ("mini-Kyoto") with blooming lotuses in summer
- 📸 **Longjing tea terraces** — White dresses against green tea fields, especially at "Shili Langdang"
- 📸 **Broken Bridge in winter** — Snow-covered bridge is the classic (but rare)

## Crowd Avoidance Strategy
1. **Start EARLY** — Arrive at Lingyin Temple before 8 AM, West Lake before 9 AM
2. **Avoid weekends and Chinese holidays** — Golden Week (Oct 1–7) and Spring Festival are packed
3. **Skip afternoon taxis** on weekends — queues can exceed 200+ people
4. **Visit popular spots at lunchtime** — Tour groups typically eat 11:30–1:00 PM
5. **Go on weekdays** whenever possible

## Local Customs & Etiquette
- 🙏 **Temples**: No photos inside temple halls. Don't step on thresholds. Dress modestly (cover shoulders).
- 🚲 **Shared bikes**: Park ONLY within white lines — ¥50 fine for improper parking in West Lake area.
- 🍵 **Tea tasting**: At Longjing Village, sample before buying. Bargain to 50–60% of initial price for street vendors. Look for official "West Lake Longjing Anti-Counterfeiting Label."
- 💬 **Language**: Basic English at major attractions. Download a translation app for menus and signs.
- 🚭 **No smoking** at most scenic spots and all indoor areas.

## Money-Saving Tips
- 💰 **West Lake is FREE** — Most causeways, gardens, and viewing spots cost nothing
- 💰 **P+R lots**: Park at ¥4–5/hr lots on outskirts + take ¥2 bus in
- 💰 **Metro**: Just ¥3–6 from city center to West Lake (Line 1, Longxiangqiao Station)
- 💰 **Shared bikes**: ~¥1.5/30 min vs ¥40 for electric carts
- 💰 **Vegetarian noodles at Lingyin**: ~¥20-30 vs ¥100+ at tourist restaurants
- 💰 **Combo ticket strategy**: Leifeng Pagoda (¥40) + boat (¥55) = under ¥100 for main attractions
- 💰 **Lingyin Temple**: Starting Dec 1, 2025 — COMPLETELY FREE (was ¥75)

## Key Apps to Download
- 📱 **Alipay / WeChat Pay** — Essential for payments everywhere
- 📱 **Amap (高德地图)** or **Baidu Maps** — More accurate than Google Maps for China
- 📱 **Didi (滴滴)** — Ride-hailing app
- 📱 **西湖智慧出行** or "西湖停车" WeChat mini-program — Real-time parking availability
- 📱 **杭州灵隐飞来峰** WeChat mini-program — Lingyin Temple reservation (required from Dec 2025)

## Quick Dos and Don'ts

|------|---------|
| Start your day before 8 AM | Drive into the scenic core area on weekends |
| Take the ¥55 boat to Three Pools | Use mosquito repellent near Lingyin Temple |
| Try Longjing tea ice cream | Take photos inside temple halls |
| Visit Leifeng Pagoda at dusk | Expect to find a taxi easily at 5 PM on weekends |
