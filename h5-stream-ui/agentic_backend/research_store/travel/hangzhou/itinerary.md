# Hangzhou One-Day Itinerary

## Recommended Route
**Lingyin Temple (Morning) → West Lake (Afternoon) → Hefang Street / Night Market (Evening)**

Most guides strongly suggest visiting Lingyin Temple first thing in the morning to avoid heavy crowds, then spending the afternoon enjoying West Lake.

---

## Morning: 7:30 AM – 12:00 PM — Lingyin Temple & Feilai Peak

| Time | Activity | Location | Details | Tips |
|------|----------|----------|---------|------|
| 7:30 | Arrive at Lingyin | Lingyin Temple Scenic Area | Beat the crowds — arrive before 9 AM | Take Bus #7 from Longxiangqiao Station or Bus #1314. Metro Line 3 to Huanglong Sports Center then special line. Avoid driving! |
| 7:30–8:30 | Feilai Peak stone carvings | Feilai Peak | Explore 300+ ancient Buddhist stone carvings (Five Dynasties to Yuan Dynasty) | Follow the stream path for the best carvings |
| 8:30–10:00 | Lingyin Temple prayer route | Central axis uphill | Heavenly King Hall → Mahavira Hall → Medicine Buddha Hall → Ji Gong Hall → Huayan Hall | No photos inside halls. Dress modestly. Don't step on thresholds. |
| 10:00–10:30 | Vegetarian noodles | Temple vegetarian hall | Famous vegetarian noodles — highly recommended | ~¥20-30 per bowl |
| 10:30–11:30 | Yongfu Temple (optional) | Exit Lingyin, turn right | "No.1 Blessed Place in Qiantang" — quieter, beautiful gardens | Free with Feilai Peak ticket |
| 11:30 | Souvenir stop | Dharma objects counter | Pick up the popular 18-seed bracelet (~¥46) | Cash recommended |

**Lingyin Temple Fees**: Feilai Peak ¥45 + Temple ¥30 = ¥75 total (FREE starting Dec 1, 2025!)

## Afternoon: 1:00 PM – 5:30 PM — West Lake Classic Route

**Recommended Route (walking + cycling)**:
> Broken Bridge → Bai Causeway → Autumn Moon Over Calm Lake → Boat to Three Pools Mirroring the Moon → Su Causeway → Viewing Fish at Flower Harbor → Leifeng Pagoda at sunset

| Time | Activity | Duration | Details |
|------|----------|----------|---------|
| 1:00 | Broken Bridge (断桥) | 30 min | Start here. White Snake legend site. Free. |
| 1:30 | Walk Bai Causeway | 20 min | Peach & willow trees. Leads to Gushan Hill. Free. |
| 1:50 | Autumn Moon Over Calm Lake (平湖秋月) | 20 min | Lakeside teahouse stop. Free. |
| 2:10 | Boat to Three Pools | 1 hr | ¥55 round trip. See the 1 RMB banknote scene! Board from nearby docks. |
| 3:10 | Su Causeway cycle/walk | 1 hr | 2.8km causeway. Rent shared bike (~¥20/hr). Free. |
| 4:10 | Viewing Fish at Flower Harbor | 30 min | Koi pond + Leifeng Pagoda photo spot. Free. |
| 4:40 | Leifeng Pagoda | 1 hr | ¥40 entrance. Take elevator to top. Sunset ~5:30 PM. Panoramic lake views! |
| 5:40 | West Lake Music Fountain | 30 min (optional) | Shows at 7 PM and 8 PM on Hubin Pedestrian Street |

**Transportation tips**:
- Shared bikes are excellent for cycling around the lake
- Electric sightseeing carts: ¥40/person around the lake
- Avoid taxis inside the scenic area — bikes and buses are more convenient
