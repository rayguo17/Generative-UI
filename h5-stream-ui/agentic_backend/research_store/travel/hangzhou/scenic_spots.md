# Hangzhou Scenic Spots — Top Attractions

## 1. Three Pools Mirroring the Moon (三潭印月)
- **Rating**: ★★★★★ (5/5) — Icon of West Lake
- **Description**: The most iconic of the "Ten Scenes of West Lake" — the image printed on the back of China's 1 RMB banknote. Three stone pagodas rise from the water; the interplay of moonlight and reflections is magical.
- **Visit Duration**: 1–1.5 hours
- **Opening Hours**: 8:00–17:00 (boat service)
- **Boat Ticket**: ¥55/person (round trip)
- **Best Time**: Early morning (after 8 AM) for beautiful light and fewer crowds
- **Tip**: Take the boat from Yigongyuan Pier or Hubin Park docks

## 2. Broken Bridge (断桥残雪)
- **Rating**: ★★★★★ (5/5) — Cultural landmark
- **Description**: The legendary meeting place of the White Snake Lady and Xu Xian. Famous for winter snow scenes but photogenic year-round. Located between Beishan Road and Bai Causeway.
- **Visit Duration**: 30–40 minutes
- **Opening Hours**: 24/7 (outdoor causeway)
- **Ticket**: Free
- **Best Tip**: Arrive before 8 AM to avoid crowds

## 3. Su Causeway (苏堤春晓)
- **Rating**: ★★★★★ (5/5) — UNESCO highlight
- **Description**: A 2.8km poetic causeway lined with weeping willows and peach blossoms in spring. Ideal for cycling or walking. Cycling from north to south is all downhill with lake on one side and mountains on the other.
- **Visit Duration**: 1–2 hours (walking) / 30–40 min (cycling)
- **Opening Hours**: 24/7
- **Ticket**: Free
- **Tip**: Rent a shared bike (~¥20/hour)

## 4. Leifeng Pagoda (雷峰塔)
- **Rating**: ★★★★★ (5/5) — Panoramic viewpoint
- **Description**: Famous from the White Snake legend. Take the elevator to the top for panoramic views of the entire West Lake. Sunset here is legendary — golden light spills across the lake.
- **Visit Duration**: 1–1.5 hours
- **Opening Hours**: 8:00–17:30 (Mar–Oct), 8:00–17:00 (Nov–Feb)
- **Entrance Fee**: ¥40/person
- **Best Time**: Dusk (golden sunset views)
- **Accessibility**: Elevator, wheelchair/stroller accessible

## 5. Curved Yard and Lotus Pool (曲院风荷)
- **Rating**: ★★★★☆ (4.5/5) — Summer highlight
- **Description**: A must-visit in summer (June–August) when the lake bursts with blooming lotuses. The red buildings here are often called "mini-Kyoto" for their aesthetic.
- **Visit Duration**: 45–60 minutes
- **Opening Hours**: 24/7 (outdoor area)
- **Ticket**: Free
- **Best Photo Spot**: Across from Liujin Bridge

## 6. Lingyin Temple (灵隐寺)
- **Rating**: ★★★★★ (5/5) — Cultural & spiritual
- **Description**: One of China's largest and most important Buddhist temples, founded in 328 AD. Features over 300 ancient stone carvings from the Five Dynasties to Yuan Dynasty. Also visit nearby Yongfu Temple (known as "No.1 Blessed Place in Qiantang").
- **Visit Duration**: 2–3 hours
- **Opening Hours**: 7:30–17:30 (last entry 17:00)
- **Entrance Fee**: ¥75 total (Feilai Peak ¥45 + Temple ¥30) — NOTE: FREE starting Dec 1, 2025
- **Best Time**: Before 9 AM to avoid crowds
- **Tip**: Try the vegetarian noodles inside the temple. Don't take photos in temple halls. Pick up the popular 18-seed bracelet (~¥46).

## 7. Autumn Moon Over the Calm Lake (平湖秋月)
- **Rating**: ★★★★☆ (4.5/5) — Evening highlight
- **Description**: On the northern shore, the premier moon-watching spot especially during the Mid-Autumn Festival. Pair the view with Longjing tea at a lakeside teahouse.
- **Visit Duration**: 30 minutes
- **Opening Hours**: 24/7
- **Ticket**: Free

## 8. Viewing Fish at Flower Harbor (花港观鱼)
- **Rating**: ★★★★☆ (4.5/5) — Serene garden
- **Description**: A serene garden where you can photograph the classic composition of koi fish + Leifeng Pagoda in one frame. The stone bridge offers the best low-angle shots.
- **Visit Duration**: 45 minutes
- **Opening Hours**: 24/7 (daylight recommended)
- **Ticket**: Free

## 9. Longjing Tea Village (龙井村)
- **Rating**: ★★★★☆ (4.5/5) — Tea culture
- **Description**: The "First Tea Village" — terraced tea gardens surrounded by mountains. Free to enter. Visit the Old Longjing Imperial Tea Garden (¥10, includes tea) to see the Eighteen Imperial Tea Trees and Longjing Spring. Try Longjing tea ice cream and watch tea roasting demonstrations.
- **Visit Duration**: 2–3 hours
- **Opening Hours**: 24/7 (village), Tea garden 8:00–16:00
- **Ticket**: Free (village), ¥10 (Old Longjing Imperial Tea Garden)
- **Best Time**: March–May (spring harvest), arrive before 9 AM or after 3 PM
- **Getting There**: Bus 27, 87, or 508H to "Longjing Tea House" stop

## 10. Hefang Street (河坊街)
- **Rating**: ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Evening street food & shopping
- **Description**: Historic pedestrian street with Ming/Qing architecture, street food, souvenir shops. Try Ding Sheng Cake, Scallion Pancake, and stinky tofu.
- **Visit Duration**: 1–2 hours
- **Opening Hours**: 9:00–22:00 (shops vary)
- **Ticket**: Free
