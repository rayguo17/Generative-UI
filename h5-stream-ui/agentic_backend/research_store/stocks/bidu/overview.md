## Business Description
Most of Baidu's revenue comes from Baidu Core, with the rest coming from video-streaming subsidiary iQiyi. The firm's main businesses include:
- **Online marketing services** from its search engine (Baidu Search)
- **AI cloud infrastructure** (Baidu AI Cloud)
- **AI applications** (Ernie Bot — large language model)
- **AI marketing** (AI-powered advertising platforms)
- **Autonomous driving** (Apollo Go robotaxi service)

Baidu is increasingly shifting its focus toward AI-powered businesses, which accounted for 35% of total revenue in Q4 2025, up from historical levels. The company is one of China's leading AI and internet technology companies.
